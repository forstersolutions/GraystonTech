01 / INTELLIGENT DELIVERY

VerityForge

Proof-governed software delivery

VerityForge coordinates specialized engineering agents through a controlled delivery system. Missions are bounded, work is isolated, proof is explicit, checks are executable, evidence is preserved, and the owner retains final release authority.

It is designed for organizations that want the speed of autonomous software work without surrendering visibility, accountability, or production judgment.

OrchestrationAgent routing, bounded missions, tool policy, and supervised runtime lanes.
ProofAcceptance contracts, verification commands, evidence capture, and audit history.
DeliveryIsolated worktrees, candidate review, release gates, and owner-controlled production decisions.

05 / PERSONNEL OPERATIONS

CUOPeration

Secure workflows for high-accountability organizations

CUOPeration translates personnel and readiness work into a secure, role-governed operating system. The public view uses fictional names and sanitized data while showing the modern signed-in administrator experience.

The platform is associated with operational work for the Ohio Army National Guard without exposing any unit designation, protected workflow, or live personnel information.

PersonnelIntake, profiles, hierarchy, approvals, roles, readiness status, and controlled records.
OperationsTasks, drill notes, notifications, assignments, private files, and action ownership.
GovernanceEncrypted records, private uploads, MFA posture, audit history, and controlled export.
